# Code of conduct

English · [简体中文](#行为准则)

Turnery follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, critique the work and not the person, and remember that many people here are learning CAD in a second language, in either direction.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and anything else a reasonable person would find unwelcoming.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository (Security → Report a vulnerability works for conduct reports too) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may ban people from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.

## 行为准则

Turnery 采用 [贡献者公约 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。简单说：友善、耐心，先假定对方是好意，就事论事不针对人；这里很多人在用第二语言学建模，中英文都是。不接受骚扰、辱骂、带性暗示的言语或图片、人身攻击、公开他人隐私，以及其他让人不舒服的言行。遇到问题，在仓库里用 GitHub 的私下报告，或通过维护者 GitHub 主页上的方式联系；报告会保密处理。
